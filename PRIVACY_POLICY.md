# Privacy Policy for Hydrafy

**Last updated: May 30, 2026**

Thank you for using Hydrafy ("the App"). Your privacy matters to us. This policy
explains what information the App handles and how.

## Summary

Hydrafy is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not require an
account, and we do not sell your personal information.

## Information We Handle

**Data stored on your device.** The App stores the information you enter and
generate while using it — such as your name, weight, activity level, climate,
reminder schedule, water intake logs, streaks, and cosmetic preferences. This
data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on
our servers.

**Notifications.** If you enable reminders, the App schedules local
notifications on your device. These are generated on-device and do not require
us to collect or store your data on a server.

**Subscriptions and purchases.** Subscriptions are processed by Apple through
the App Store. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to manage and validate subscriptions. To
do this, RevenueCat and Apple may process information such as your purchase
history, subscription status, and anonymous device identifiers. We do not
receive your name, email, or payment card details from these purchases. Please
review the privacy policies of:
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:
- Provide the App's core features (tracking, goals, reminders, streaks).
- Process and restore your subscription.
- Keep the App working and secure.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with the service
providers needed to operate the App (Apple and RevenueCat) as described above,
or where required by law.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data you create in the App stays on your device. You can delete it at any time
using the "Reset all data" option in the App's Settings, or by deleting the App
from your device. Subscription records held by Apple and RevenueCat are governed
by their respective policies.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page
with an updated "Last updated" date.
