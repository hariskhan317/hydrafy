Hydrafy makes drinking enough water simple and genuinely fun.

Hydrafy is a subscription app. A paid subscription (Hydrafy Pro) is required to use the app and unlocks all features below. Every new subscription starts with a 3-day free trial — cancel anytime before it ends and you won't be charged.

Set a smart daily goal based on your body, activity, and climate — then log each glass with a tap and watch your hydration buddy come to life as you fill up.

Your Hydrafy Pro subscription includes:
• Personalized daily goal from your weight, activity, and climate
• One-tap logging for glasses, bottles, or custom amounts
• Gentle sip reminders that fit your daily schedule
• Streaks that keep you motivated day after day
• Weekly history and clear progress insights
• All mascot cosmetics, unlocked

Stay consistent, feel better, and make hydration something you actually look forward to.

— Subscription details —
Hydrafy requires a paid subscription to use. All features are part of the subscription; there is no free tier beyond the introductory trial.

• Hydrafy Pro is available as Monthly or Yearly, each with a 3-day free trial
• Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase
• Subscription auto-renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period
• Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period
• Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings
