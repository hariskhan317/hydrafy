## Frequently Asked Questions

**How is my daily water goal calculated?**
Your goal is based on your weight, activity level, and climate, which you set
during onboarding. You can adjust your daily goal anytime in Settings.

**How do I log water?**
On the Today screen, tap a quick-add tile (glass or bottle) or use "custom" to
enter any amount. To remove an entry you added by mistake, long-press it in the
Today list.

**How do reminders work?**
Hydrafy sends gentle on-device reminders during your waking hours. You can turn
reminders on/off or pause them for the day in Settings.

**What does the subscription include?**
A subscription unlocks full access to Hydrafy. Plans include Pro Monthly and
Pro Yearly, and start with a 3-day free trial.

**How do I restore my subscription?**
If you reinstalled the App or got a new device, open the paywall and tap
"Restore purchases" to restore your active subscription.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name →
Subscriptions → Hydrafy → Cancel. Your access continues until the end of the
current billing period.

**Will resetting my data cancel my subscription?**
No. "Reset all data" only clears your local progress on the device. Your
subscription is tied to your Apple ID and is not affected.

**How do I delete my data?**
Go to Settings → "Reset all data", or delete the App from your device.
